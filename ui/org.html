Overview

Company structure

Every service belongs somewhere. ArchGuard maps the company once — organization, departments, sub-departments, teams, services — so a rule written high up automatically applies to everything underneath it. Write a rule once for Payments, and every team and service in Payments inherits it.

Organization
Rules cascade down

A rule attaches at one level and applies to everything beneath it:

  • Org — applies to the whole company
  • Department — just that department
  • Team — just that team's services

A team can add stricter rules for itself. It can't loosen one it inherited — that needs a tracked, time-boxed violation.

How you set this up

Commit one small file. ArchGuard maps each service to its place using your existing CODEOWNERS, so you don't maintain it twice.

organization: Northwind Retail Group
departments:
  - name: Payments
    architect: j.okafor
    sub_departments:
      - name: Checkout
        lead: d.kim
        teams:
          - name: Checkout Core
            services: [checkout-api, checkout-web]
