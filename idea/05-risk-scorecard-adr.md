# Idea 5: Executive Risk Scorecard and ADR Generator

## One-line pitch

ArchGuard AI converts architecture review findings into a risk scorecard and Architecture Decision Record so engineering leaders can track architectural health over time.

## Problem

PR-level architecture violations are useful, but leaders also need trend visibility. Which teams repeatedly violate service ownership? Which domains accumulate coupling risk? Which exceptions were approved and why?

## Proposed experience

- Every architecture review produces structured findings.
- Findings roll up into a scorecard by domain, service, policy, and severity.
- When a team intentionally accepts a deviation, ArchGuard AI drafts an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) with context, decision, consequences, and expiry date.
- Leaders can see architecture risk burn-down across sprints.

## Demo story

After the PR violation is caught, the demo shows a scorecard:

- Coupling risk increased in Order domain.
- One high-severity direct database access violation blocked.
- Recommended remediation: event-driven Inventory update flow.
- Draft ADR available if the team wants a time-boxed exception.

## MVP scope

- Generate a markdown scorecard artifact from sample findings.
- Include trend placeholders rather than a full database.
- Generate one ADR draft for an approved exception scenario.

## Wow factor

The product speaks both developer and executive languages: PR comments for engineers, architecture risk posture for leaders.

## Why judges will care

- **Impact:** makes architecture governance measurable.
- **Feasibility:** starts from the same findings generated by the PR review.
- **Novelty:** connects CI/CD architecture checks to decision governance.
- **Enterprise value:** shows how the idea scales beyond a single demo PR.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Risk:** scorecards can become vanity metrics.
  - **Mitigation:** focus on actionable counts: blocked high-risk changes, approved exceptions, recurring policy violations.
- **Risk:** ADR generation may create documentation noise.
  - **Mitigation:** generate ADRs only for accepted exceptions or high-impact decisions.
